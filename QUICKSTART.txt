AUDIO ROUTER - WINDOWS 11 (22H2 OR LATER), 64-BIT

Double-click AudioRouter.exe. No .NET installation is needed.
The Setup guide button shows numbered steps and links to Windows settings.

ONE-TIME SETUP
1. Download VB-CABLE from https://vb-audio.com/Cable/
   Extract the ZIP. Run VBCABLE_Setup_x64.exe as administrator.
   Restart Windows if requested.
2. Keep your normal speakers/headphones as Windows' output device and your
   usual microphone as its input device. The driver installer may change these.
3. In Audio Router, click Refresh. Choose your microphone and CABLE Input.
4. In your destination game/app's Audio, Voice, or Microphone settings, choose
   CABLE Output (VB-Audio Virtual Cable) as input, then save or apply.
   Alternatively: Windows Settings > System > Sound > Volume mixer > Apps.
   Expand the destination app and choose CABLE Output as Input device,
   where that selector is available. Keep Output on your normal speakers.
   If the app is missing, open it and start playback or voice chat.
   If Windows routing is ignored or unavailable, use the app's own mic selector.
   Some apps need to restart after a device change.
5. Use the destination app's microphone test, or confirm with another listener
   that both your voice and the selected application audio are present.

EVERYDAY USE
Select Brave, Spotify, or another running app. Click Start routing.
Play audio in that application. Enable the destination app's microphone.
Both the selected app and your voice reach its virtual microphone.

- The app's audio keeps playing through your normal speakers/headphones.
- Windows volume keys control what YOU hear. Music sent to others is separate.
- Music sent to others and Your voice sliders control the transmitted mix.
- Music sent to others supports 0-200%: 100% is the original captured level;
  200% doubles the outgoing signal (+6 dB) without changing your listening level.
- Microphone and cable dropdowns work while routing is live. Changing either
  briefly restarts routing, keeping the app selection, levels, and mute/pause.
  If the new device is unavailable, routing stops so you can choose another.
- Use CABLE Input with CABLE Output in the destination app. The separate CABLE In 16ch
  requires CABLE Out 16ch there; the app shows the matching device name.
- Uncheck Share application audio to pause music; your microphone continues.
- Closing the window with X hides it to the tray; voice and music keep working.
  Click the tray icon or launch the executable again to restore the same instance.
- Click Stop routing, or right-click the tray icon > Exit Audio Router,
  to stop BOTH music and voice. Tray Exit also quits the app completely.
  Change the destination app's input back to your real microphone to talk
  without Audio Router, or reopen Audio Router and start routing again.
- Browser selection includes all audible tabs and windows in that browser.
- Source apps that close need to be selected again after reopening.
- Click Refresh after opening an app or connecting a device. Lists do not
  refresh automatically in the background.
- Enable Live meters for bars and live dB numbers. Off by default; the setting
  is saved. When off, peak measurement and UI meter updates stop. Metering
  also pauses in the tray or while minimized; slider percentages stay visible.

IF SOMETHING IS SILENT
Enable Live meters to check microphone, music, and combined output.
Allow desktop apps microphone access in Windows Privacy settings.
Make sure the destination app uses the matching CABLE Output microphone.
Protected audio and some applications may not support process capture.
The source application's own playback volume can affect the captured copy.
Receiving apps may compress music or suppress it as background noise.

Use headphones to avoid your microphone picking up speaker audio twice.
Leave Windows "Listen to this device" OFF for the cable.
Do not set the cable as your default Windows speaker or microphone.

PRIVACY AND REMOVAL
Audio is processed locally in memory; it is not recorded or uploaded.
Settings and error logs: %LOCALAPPDATA%\AudioRouter
The app never changes Windows audio defaults, endpoint volume, per-app output
assignments, or destination-app configuration.
To remove the app, choose tray Exit, then delete this folder. Optionally delete settings.
Remove VB-CABLE separately with its vendor installer if no longer needed.
